## How to Use It

1. Start with the [decision matrix](./framework/hybrid-agile-model.md#decision-matrix-which-method-to-apply) to pick the method that fits each team.
2. Run your events with the [ceremonies guide](./ceremonies/ceremonies-guide.md); each section links to its template.
3. Add AI help one workflow at a time, after your team agrees the [guardrails](./ai-workflows/ai-guardrails.md).
4. Copy the [templates](./templates/README.md) into your own tools and adapt them.

## Core Philosophy

Modern delivery is not purely Scrum, not purely Kanban, and not purely AI-driven. It is a deliberate blend chosen to fit the team, the product, and the moment.

This playbook documents the approach I have refined across 9+ years and 5+ companies:
- **Scrum** for product development squads with clear sprint goals
- **Nexus** when scaling beyond a single team
- **Kanban** for operations, ITSM, and support workflows
- **AI** as a force multiplier — not a replacement — for PM judgment
