## Core Philosophy

Modern delivery is not purely Scrum, not purely Kanban, and not purely AI-driven. It is a deliberate blend chosen to fit the team, the product, and the moment.

This playbook documents the approach I have refined across 9+ years and 5+ companies:
- **Scrum** for product development squads with clear sprint goals
- **Nexus** when scaling beyond a single team
- **Kanban** for operations, ITSM, and support workflows
- **AI** as a force multiplier — not a replacement — for PM judgment
